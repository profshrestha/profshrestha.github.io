Consulting

Robotics, embedded AI, and sensing. I work with companies, institutions, and legal teams that need a technical judgement they can rely on, from someone who builds these systems rather than only writes about them.

I direct the Intelligent Systems Lab at Sonoma State University, where my students and I build robot manipulation systems, embedded sensing devices, and the machine learning that runs on both. The lab runs ten robot stations with six-axis research arms and the GPU hardware to train policies on them.

That work is the basis of what I consult on. I take a small number of engagements where a specific technical problem needs depth rather than headcount, and where being wrong is expensive: a sensing approach that will or will not reach the accuracy the product claims, an automation plan that will or will not survive contact with a real line, a patent whose claims need someone who can read both the mathematics and the circuit.

Areas of expertise

Each of these is an area I publish and build in, not one I have read about.

Robotics and manipulation

Robot arms that have to do real work in unstructured settings, from the kinematics upward.

  • Manipulator kinematics, Jacobians, and workspace analysis
  • Six-axis arm selection, integration, and ROS 2 deployment
  • Teleoperation and demonstration capture
  • Grasping, contact, and end-effector design
  • Feasibility review before a cell is bought or built

Robot learning

Getting a policy from a dataset onto hardware, and knowing when that is the wrong approach.

  • Imitation learning and learning from demonstration
  • Reinforcement learning for manipulation
  • Sim-to-real transfer and the gap that remains
  • Vision-language-action systems
  • Honest assessment of what current methods will and will not do

Embedded systems and edge AI

Inference and sensing on hardware with a power budget, where a cloud round trip is not available.

  • Microcontroller sensor platforms and RTOS design
  • On-device inference and model compression
  • Low-power and battery-constrained systems
  • Sensor fusion and signal conditioning
  • Data logging, telemetry, and cellular-connected devices

Biomedical sensing and health AI

Measurement problems in health, including the non-invasive ones that are hard for good physical reasons.

  • Breath volatile organic compound sensing
  • Non-invasive physiological measurement
  • Wearable and at-home data collection devices
  • Study design and data pipelines for clinical collaboration
  • Machine learning on small, noisy biomedical datasets

Computer vision and perception

Seeing well enough to act, which is a different requirement from scoring well on a benchmark.

  • Depth sensing, point clouds, and 3D registration
  • Camera calibration and hand-eye calibration
  • Deep learning for classification and detection
  • Medical image analysis
  • Visual servoing and closed-loop perception

RF and radar sensing

Sensing through materials and in conditions where optical methods stop working.

  • FMCW radar system design and signal processing
  • Through-wall and obscured-environment detection
  • Antenna and RF front-end review
  • Microwave and millimetre-wave measurement

Technical review and expert work

Where the deliverable is a defensible opinion rather than a prototype.

  • Patent and prior-art technical review
  • Expert witness and litigation support
  • Due diligence on technology claims for investors
  • Proposal and grant technical review
  • Independent assessment of a vendor's technical claims

Education and workforce

Building the capability inside an organisation rather than renting it.

  • Workshops on robotics, ROS 2, and applied machine learning
  • Curriculum and lab design for institutions
  • Capstone project sponsorship and student pipelines
  • Technical training for engineering teams

How engagements work

Four shapes cover nearly everything. If your problem does not fit one, say so and we will find the right shape for it.

Technical advisory

An ongoing relationship with a small, regular time commitment. Suited to a team that wants a second opinion available as decisions come up, rather than a report at the end.

Project engagement

A scoped piece of work with a defined deliverable: a feasibility study, a prototype, a system design, or an evaluation of an approach you are considering.

Expert review

A written technical opinion for patent, litigation, due diligence, or procurement, delivered to the standard that context requires.

Training and workshops

Delivered at your site or in the lab, from a half-day introduction through a multi-week programme for an engineering team.

A note on availability and scope. My primary appointment is at Sonoma State University, and consulting is undertaken consistently with university policy on outside professional activity. I take on a limited number of engagements at a time, which is deliberate: it is what lets each one get real attention.
